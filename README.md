### 📋 Project Overview

Comprehensive **sales performance analysis** of a pizza store using a real-world pizza sales dataset. The project focuses on extracting key business metrics (KPIs), identifying sales trends, and providing actionable insights to support data-driven decision making.

**Objective**: Analyze sales data to understand revenue performance, customer ordering behavior, and product popularity.

---

### 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- **Database**: PostgreSQL (pgAdmin)
- **Data Cleaning & Visualization**: Microsoft Excel
- **Query Language**: SQL
- **Data Source**: `pizza_sales.xlsx`

### 🔍 Analysis Performed

#### 1. Business KPI Dashboard (Excel)
- Created a clean, professional KPI summary dashboard
- Used formulas and formatting for clear business reporting

#### 2. Sales Trend Analysis (PostgreSQL)
- **Daily Trend**: Identified peak and low-performing days (Wednesday = highest orders)
- **Hourly Trend**: Peak ordering hours (5 PM & 12 PM)

#### 3. Product Performance
- Sales contribution by **Pizza Category** (Classic, Supreme, Veggie)
- Sales distribution by **Pizza Size** (Large, Medium, Regular, etc.)
- **Top 5 Best Sellers** and **Bottom 5 Worst Sellers**

---

### 💡 Key Insights

- **Wednesday** is the busiest day, while **Friday** has the lowest orders.
- Most orders are placed during **lunch (12 PM)** and **dinner (5 PM)** hours.
- **Classic** category dominates sales (~62.6%).
- **Large** and **Regular** sizes are the most popular.
- **The Hawaiian Pizza**, **The Pepperoni Pizza**, and **The Big Meat Pizza** are top performers.
